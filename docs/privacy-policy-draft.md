BabyCare is a private journal that families and caregivers share to keep track of a baby's day. It is
meant for parents and caregivers aged 18 or over. It is not for children, and it is not a medical
device or a source of medical advice.

## What we collect

**Your account**
- Your email address and the name you choose (or that Google provides if you sign in with Google).
- If you sign in with Google, Google also gives us your Google profile picture address. We do not show or use it.
- If you use an email and password, your password is handled by Firebase Authentication. We never see it.

**Your baby and your family room** (you choose what to enter)
- Your baby's name, nickname, date of birth, optional time of birth, optional gender, optional notes, and an optional profile photo.
- The family room's name, and each member's name and role (owner, parent, caregiver or viewer).
- Temporary invitation codes, and a counter of failed code attempts, so codes can't be guessed.

**What you log**
- Feeding, diaper, sleep, bath, outdoor-time and note entries: when they happened, how long they lasted, details you add (such as feeding type, amount or a diaper type), free-text notes, who logged them, and an optional photo.

**Your settings**
- Time zone, which activities you track, how your home screen is arranged, and reminder times and labels you set.

**On your phone only (not sent to us)**
- Reminders are scheduled on your phone. Shake-to-log reads the motion sensor on the phone and stores nothing.
- The camera is used only to scan an invitation QR code. The picture is not saved or sent.
- Photos are only read when you choose one to attach.

## What we do not collect

We do not collect your location, contacts, microphone audio, or advertising ID, and we do not use
analytics or advertising tools at this time. [UPDATE THIS SECTION BEFORE ADDING ADS, ANALYTICS OR PURCHASES.]

## How we use it

Only to run the app for your family room: to sign you in, show your journal to the people you invited,
sync it between phones, keep photos private to the room, and protect the service from abuse. We do not
sell your information and we do not use your family's entries or photos for advertising.

## Who can see it

- **People in your family room** can see the baby profile, entries and photos, according to their role. Viewers can look but not change anything. The room's owner chooses who joins.
- **Our service provider.** Your data is stored and processed by Google (Firebase: Authentication, Realtime Database, Cloud Storage and Cloud Functions) on our behalf. The database and functions run in Singapore (Google Cloud region asia-southeast1). Photos are stored in [STORAGE LOCATION, CHECK IN THE FIREBASE CONSOLE].
- We share information if the law requires it.

## How we protect it

Data travels over encrypted connections. Access rules on the server let only a room's members read its data, and only the right roles change it. Photos are private to the room. No system is perfectly secure; tell us at once if you think your account has been misused.

## How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep your information until you delete it.
- **Delete your account:** Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, sign-in and settings and removes you from your family rooms. Entries you logged stay in your family's journal under the name you used, because they belong to the family.
- **Delete a family room:** the owner can delete the room, which permanently deletes its babies, entries and photos for everyone. A room's owner must do this before deleting their account.
- **Delete a photo or baby:** removes it from the room.
- You can also ask us to delete your data at [PRIVACY EMAIL] or [WEB ADDRESS OF DELETION REQUEST PAGE]. [GOOGLE PLAY REQUIRES A WEB ADDRESS FOR THIS.]
- Google may keep encrypted backups for a limited time after deletion. [CONFIRM THE CURRENT PERIOD.]

## Children

BabyCare is for adults. A baby's details are entered by their parent or caregiver, who is responsible for choosing what to share. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.
